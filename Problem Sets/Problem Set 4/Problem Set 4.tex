\item Find the max-norm condition number, $\kappa_{\infty}(A)$, of the following matrices.

\item Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ non-singular matrix and $a \neq 0$ a real number.

\begin{enumerate}[(\roman{enumii})]

\item What is the norm of a matrix $\alpha A$ in terms of the norm of $A$?

\color{TangGreen}
The norm of a matrix $\alpha A$ should be $\alpha$ times the norm of $A$. 
\color{black}

\item What is the inverse of the matrix $\alpha A$?

\color{TangGreen}
Given that the inverse of a matrix $A$ is $A^{-1}$, the inverse of $\alpha A$ should then be $\frac{1}{\alpha} A^{-1}$. 
\color{black}

\item What is the condition number of $\alpha A$ in terms of the condition number of $A$?

\color{TangGreen}
The condition number of $\alpha A$ would require the norm of the inverse of $\alpha A$. This would be $\frac{1}{\alpha}$ times the norm of $A^{-1}$. This means the condition number of $\alpha A$ would be the same as the norm of $A$, given that $\alpha ||A|| * \frac{1}{\alpha} ||A^{-1}||$ would just result in $||A|| * ||A^{-1}||$. 
\color{black}

\end{enumerate}

Relative forward error is calculated by $\frac{||x - x_{a}||_{\infty}}{||x||_{\infty}}$, relative backward error is calculated by $\frac{||b - Ax_{a}||_{\infty}}{||b||_{\infty}}$, and EMF is calculated by dividing relative forward error by relative backward error.
