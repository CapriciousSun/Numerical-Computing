\noindent\textbf{1. }(10 pts) (Hand computations.)

\begin{enumerate}[\textbf{(\alph{enumi})}]

\item Find the LU factorizations of the following matrix. Check your answe by multiplication: $A = LU$.
$$A = \begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 2 \\
4 & 3 & 7 \\
6 & 7 & 22 
\end{bmatrix}$$

\color{TangGreen}
Using Gaussian elimination, the upper matrix could be formed in the following process
\begin{gather*}
\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 2 \\
4 & 3 & 3 \\
6 & 7 & 22
\end{bmatrix} \rightarrow \text{subtract } 2 \times \text{row 1 from row 2} \rightarrow \begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & 3 \\
6  & 7 & 22 
\end{bmatrix} \rightarrow \text{subtract }3 \times \text{row 1 from row 2} \rightarrow \begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 16 
\end{bmatrix} \\
\rightarrow \text{subtract row 2 from row 3} \rightarrow \begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & 3 \\
0 & 0 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
\end{gather*}
From these operations, the lower triangluar matrix could be formed as 
$$L = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
2 & 1 & 0 \\
3 & 4 & 1 
\end{bmatrix}$$
Verifying for it
$$A = LU = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
2 & 1 & 0 \\
3 & 4  & 1 
\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & 3 \\
0 & 0 & 4 
\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 2 \\
4 & 3 & 7 \\
6 & 7 & 22 
\end{bmatrix}$$
\color{black}

\item Solve the following system by first finding the LU factorization and then solving by forward elimination and back-substitution. Show the intermediate steps.

\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
x_{1} \\
x_{2} \\
x_{3} 
\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
32 \\
-8 \\
11 
\end{bmatrix}$$

\end{enumerate}
