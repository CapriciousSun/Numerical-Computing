\begin{gather*}
\begin{bmatrix}

2 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & 3 \\
6  & 7 & 22 
\end{bmatrix} \rightarrow \text{subtract }3 \times \text{row 1 from row 2} \rightarrow \begin{bmatrix}
2 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 1 & 3 \\

\end{gather*}

Verifying for it
$$A = LU = \begin{bmatrix}

$$\left[ \begin{array}{c c c | c}

For (i), the max norm $||A||_{\infty} = -5$ and for (ii), the max norm $||A||_{\infty} = -7$. For (i), a vector $x$ that has $||x||_{\infty} = 1$ and $||Ax||_{\infty} = ||A||_{\infty}$ is the vector $\begin{bmatrix}

\item Find the max-norm condition number, $\kappa_{\infty}(A)$, of the following matrices.
$$(i)\ \ \ A = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4 
\end{bmatrix},\ \ \ (ii)\ \ \ A = \begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2.01 \\
2 & 4 
\end{bmatrix}$$

\color{TangGreen}
To calculate the max-norm condition numbers, the inverse of each matrix is needed. For (i), $A^{-1} = \frac{1}{4 - 6}\begin{bmatrix}
4 & -2 \\
-3 & 1 
\end{bmatrix} = -\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix}
4 & -2 \\
-3 & 1 
\end{bmatrix}$. So the condition number is $\text{cond}(A) = ||A||_{\infty} * ||A^{-1}||_{\infty} = 4 * -2 = -8$. For (ii), $A^{-1} = \frac{1}{4 - 4.02}\begin{bmatrix}
4 & -2.01 \\
-2 & 1 
\end{bmatrix}$, so the condition number is $\text{cond}(A) = ||A||_{\infty} * ||A||_{\infty} = 4 * -0.08 = -0.32$.
